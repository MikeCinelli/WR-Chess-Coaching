About

The Coach

My name is Michael Cinelli. I’m a Class A chess player rated 1800 with extensive teaching experience. I began playing chess with my dad at age five and have been playing seriously for the past ten years. I want to turn my hometown of Wood-Ridge into a chess powerhouse.

Teaching Experience

I was the full-time head chess coach at Success Academy Charter Schools’ Hell’s Kitchen Elementary School. I taught over 200 students and managed three after-school clubs, including an elite, tournament-winning traveling club of 30 selected students. I also taught piano at School of Rock Carlstadt.

Methodology

I offer private, hour-long chess lessons in your home. During lessons, we’ll study chess principles, play and analyze games, and more. Between lessons, you can challenge me to an online game, which I’ll also play and analyze. Once enough students sign up, I plan to create and run the Wood-Ridge Chess Club.

Why Chess?

Chess Is Fun

Chess is a thrilling game despite its reputation. Speed chess can be as exciting as any game, while longer games can make time fly by as your mind becomes absorbed in the position. In the words of the great Judit Polgár, “Giving checkmate is always fun.”

More Than a Game

Chess encourages important life skills: personal accountability, patience, decision-making under pressure, and more. In many ways, chess is a study in self-improvement itself. Chess is also widely associated with intelligence and strategic thinking.
